Meet Dr Manu

Healing words, both ways

Dr Manu Manjith is a homoeopathic physician with a special interest in psychiatry and counselling. Born in Ulliyeri, Kozhikode, he has always kept medicine and writing side by side.

Since his film debut with Ohm Shanthi Oshaana in 2014, he has written songs for more than eighty Malayalam films, from Jacobinte Swargarajyam and Minnal Murali to RDX and ARM, often with composer Shaan Rahman. Audiences call him the Doctor Lyricist.

That ear for feeling shapes how he consults: patiently, without judgement, and always starting with what you want to say.

New to online counselling?

Everything you need to know before your first session.

Is the session really private?

Yes. The video call happens one-to-one in a private room on this website, and the doctor admits you from a waiting room. What you share stays between you and the doctor.

Do I need to install an app?

No. The call opens in your browser. Chrome or Safari on a phone or laptop works best.

How do I get the link?

Once your booking is confirmed you get an email and a WhatsApp message with your consultation link, plus reminders 24 hours and 1 hour before.

Can I consult from outside India?

Yes. Book the same way. If you can't pay by UPI, message us on WhatsApp for other options.

Can I change or cancel my time?

Yes. Use the “Change or cancel” link in your confirmation, or message us on WhatsApp.

Is this for emergencies?

No. Online sessions are not for emergencies. If you or someone near you is in danger, contact local emergency services or go to the nearest hospital.
