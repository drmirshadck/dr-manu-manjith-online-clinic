Dr Manu ManjithBHMS, MD (Psychiatry)

Dr Manu Manjith is a homoeopathic physician with a postgraduate degree in psychiatry and over 15 years of clinical experience. He is an Assistant Professor in the Department of Psychiatry at Father Muller Homoeopathic Medical College, Mangalore.

Alongside medicine, he is an established Malayalam film lyricist. Both journeys have shaped his interest in human behaviour and emotion, and how people express themselves.
